Deployed Prototype · Kenya SDG 9 · Industry & Innovation

🚧 AI Barabara — See a pothole. Get a decision.

Problem: Road defects go unrepaired because inspection is slow, subjective, and disconnected from funding decisions.

Solution: A working prototype built with the Kenya Urban Roads Authority (KURA). Computer vision detects road defects from a single photograph; an HDM-4 economic engine returns NPV, benefit-cost ratio, and IRR in under five seconds. A qualified engineer reviews and approves every output before any work is scheduled.

Offline Classroom AI · AGPL-3.0 SDG 4 · Quality Education

🎓 AI Teaching Assistant — Stop the Silence

Problem: In large classes, students who don't understand stay silent — fear, shyness, or intimidation hides gaps until exams expose them.

Solution: A private AI tutor, built by a lecturer and TSC-registered educator, that lets students ask anonymously, 24/7, with no fear of judgment. It runs entirely on your laptop — no internet needed during class — and answers from your own course materials. A live analytics dashboard shows exactly which concepts the class didn't understand, so you can intervene in real time.

Research Prototype · Nyeri SDG 2 · Zero Hunger

🍃 AI-Powered Early Warning for Coffee Leaf Rust

Problem: Farmers in Nyeri lose up to 80% of their yield to coffee leaf rust, and current warnings arrive only after outbreaks have taken hold.

Solution: A locally-deployed, interpretable model that predicts coffee rust outbreaks up to seven days in advance. Trained on 9,850 KALRO samples and validated in Nyeri, it delivers risk assessments via SMS, WhatsApp, or web — empowering farmers with data-driven decisions before outbreaks occur.

Accuracy 77.6% AUC-ROC 0.867 Nyeri validation 74.6%

Team: 2 CS PhD · 1 agricultural scientist · 1 data analyst · research assistants - DeKUT + Nyeri County Government.

Research Platform · KENET-funded SDG 3 · Good Health

🔬 Towards Smarter, Continuous Urinalysis

Problem: Routine urinalysis misses diagnostically relevant objects — and each missed object is a missed opportunity for early intervention.

Solution: An evolving research platform using image processing and AI to improve microscopic urinalysis — detecting overlooked objects, assessing diagnostic impact, and building longitudinal patient diagnostic histories. Supported by a KENET grant (2023–2024) for early disease detection and monitoring.
